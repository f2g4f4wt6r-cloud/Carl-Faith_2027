FAQS

WHAT IS THE DRESS CODE?

We would love to see you in your best modern Filipiniana, Barong, or summer casual attire. We kindly ask guests to avoid slippers, jeans, and rubber shoes.

ARE PLUS ONES ALLOWED?

As much as we would love to accommodate everyone, plus ones are allowed only if explicitly stated in the invitation. We want to keep our wedding small and intimate.

IS THERE PARKING?

Yes. There are allotted parking spaces available at the venue.

IS THE WEDDING INDOORS OR OUTDOORS?

Our wedding will be an outdoor, covered celebration. Since our wedding is in April, please expect warm summer weather and a lovely breeze. 🌿

WHEN IS THE RSVP DEADLINE?

Kindly confirm your attendance by October 31, 2026.

CAN I TAKE PHOTOS DURING THE CEREMONY?

We'd love for you to capture the memories, but we kindly ask that you keep your phones away during the ceremony so our professional photographers can capture the moments without distractions.

WHAT SHOULD I BRING FOR AN OUTDOOR WEDDING?

Since we're celebrating outdoors in April, feel free to bring a fan, umbrella, sunglasses, or anything that will help keep you comfortable in the summer weather.

DO YOU HAVE A GIFT REGISTRY?

Your presence is more than enough. If you wish to bless us with a gift, monetary gifts are sincerely appreciated and will help us as we begin our life together.
