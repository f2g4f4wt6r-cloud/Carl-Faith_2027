✿

As much as we would love to accommodate everyone, this celebration is strictly limited to the guests named on the invitation. We kindly ask that you do not bring a plus one.

♡

We kindly encourage our guests to follow our wedding color pallete: shades of pink and green. Guests are encouraged to wear any shade within these colors that they feel comfortable and confident in.

Kindly avoid white, ivory, champagne, or colors closely resembling the bridal gown.Other than that have fun with the pink and green pallete!

Our wedding will be an outdoor celebration. Since our wedding is in April, please expect warm summer weather and beautiful blooms.🌿

✿

Kindly confirm your attendance by January 31, 2027. We would appreciate receiving your RSVP before the deadline so we can prepare everything especially for you.

We'd love for you to capture the memories, but we kindly ask that you keep your phones away during the ceremony so our professional photographers can capture the moments without distractions.

✦

Since we're celebrating outdoors in April, feel free to bring a mini fan, and sunglasses, to keep you comfortable in the summer weather.

Your presence is more than enough. If you wish to bless us with a gift, monetary gifts are sincerely appreciated and will help us as we begin our life together.
